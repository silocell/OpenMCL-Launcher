This file makes OpenMCL portable.

While it sits next to OpenMCL.exe, the launcher keeps everything — your
instances, worlds, mods, settings and signed-in accounts — in the
OpenMCL-Data folder beside it, rather than in your Windows user profile.

That means you can run OpenMCL from a USB stick on any computer and take
your worlds with you, leaving nothing behind on the machine.

Delete this file and OpenMCL will store its data in %APPDATA%\OpenMCL like a
normal installation. (If OpenMCL-Data already exists beside the executable it
keeps using that, so you won't lose anything by deleting this file.)
